TITLE

Your Style Light


TAGLINE

A clean analog face with weather, moon phase and date.


DESCRIPTION

Your Style Light is the easy-going sibling of Your Style: a classic analog dial with Roman numerals at XII, III, VI and IX, bold rounded hands and a small centre hub, ready to wear without touching a single setting.

Inside the dial you find everything you glance at during the day. At the top a weather symbol with today's high and low temperature. On the left the current moon phase. On the right the weekday and the date in a small date window. At the bottom your steps for today, with a slim bar that fills up towards your daily goal.

The weather symbols are drawn with soft edges and take on the colours of your theme. There are twelve of them, from clear sky and partly cloudy (each in a day and a night version) to fog, drizzle, rain, showers, snow, sleet and thunderstorms.

Black on white is the default. Ten colour templates are one tap away in the settings: Classic, Noir, Swiss, Ivory, Blush, Navy, Ocean, Forest, Plum and Sport. Each template is only a starting point, every colour can be changed on its own afterwards.

Pebble Round 2 and Pebble Time 2 each get their own layout: numerals and ticks follow the round rim on one and the rectangular edge on the other.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo for your current location, every 30 minutes. No account and no API key are needed. The face redraws once a minute.


SETTINGS

- Template - Classic, Noir, Swiss, Ivory, Blush, Navy, Ocean, Forest, Plum, Sport or Custom.
- Colours - background, numerals and ticks, hour hand, minute hand, centre hub, text and weather symbol, highlights, and both sides of the moon.
- Style - minute track, solid, hollow or inlaid hands, frame around the date, ring around the moon, step goal bar and daily step goal.
- Show - weather, moon phase, date, steps and hour ticks, each on or off.
- Language - English or German weekdays and labels.
- Weather - current conditions or today's forecast as the symbol; temperature in Celsius, Fahrenheit or automatically by phone region.
